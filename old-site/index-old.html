Career switch: full-time data analytics

Bangalore, IndiaApr 2023 – Dec 2023AnalytixLabs

Working with data every day at Itilite showed me how much decisions depend on it. I tried to learn analytics alongside my job, but with no technical background I couldn't get the basics to stick. So in January 2023 I resigned to study full-time and rebuild from first principles.

I also worked through the Python track: 8 notebooks covering pandas, Seaborn and SciPy, from Python basics through retail, credit-card, insurance-claims and hypothesis-testing case studies to an end-to-end e-commerce marketing capstone (8-table merge, acquisition and retention, segmentation, delivery and ratings).

Result: Certified Data Analyst (Term 1: Excel, SQL & Power BI), AnalytixLabs. Within weeks of finishing, I joined Forbes Advisor in data research.

Two capstones pulled the tools together: an end-to-end retail customer analysis (SQL Server cleaning and 15 business queries → Excel pivots and Pareto → a 3-page Power BI dashboard) and a supply-chain analysis in Python on 180,000 orders. It found that 57% of deliveries were late, and that the premium shipping modes were the least reliable.
