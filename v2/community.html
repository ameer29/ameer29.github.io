PEER Community Programme

Six months as a volunteer mentor and group lead with MENDAKI Club, Feb – Aug 2026: the programme, the 5Cs framework, and every session of the season.

Volunteer Mentor · PEER Community Programme

PEER @ Tampines, SingaporeFeb – Aug 2026 · completedGroup leadMENDAKI Club · Yayasan MENDAKI

PEER (Positive Engagement through Experiential Relationships) is a six-month programme that pairs trained volunteer mentors with secondary-school students aged 11–16. It runs every other Saturday, built on the 5Cs of Positive Youth Development. I mentored through the full Feb – Aug 2026 cycle, from mentor onboarding and mock rounds on 7 February to the closing session in August.

Every session · Feb – Aug 2026
The check-in: one happy moment
We opened every session by asking each mentee for one moment that made them happy in the previous week. Over the season it became the way in: the mentees got fully involved and began opening up about whatever was worrying them.

My own observations: Sessions 1–4 from my placement report, the rest of the season from my experience as group lead.

Through a psychology lens
  • Rapport can't be rushed. As in therapy, the relationship is the intervention, so I built trust through consistency rather than questions.
  • Play and art lower the barrier. Adolescents who won't talk in a new group will draw, build and play, and the conversation follows.
  • Feedback loops keep it honest. Mentee journals and mentor reports to group leads mean the programme adapts to what the mentees actually need.

My placement report, written at the end of April, looks closely at Sessions 1–4. I stayed with my squad as group lead for the rest of the season, through to the closing session on 1 August.
