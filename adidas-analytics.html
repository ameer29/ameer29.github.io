Adidas: where the growth really is

A Tableau study of Adidas sales and profitability by region, product, retailer and sales channel. It found that the biggest revenue engine isn't where the best margins are, and it ends with a plan to test the recommendations before scaling them.

My contribution · Dashboards

Analysing the business from four angles

What the data said

Five findings

  • High sales doesn't mean high margin. The West is the revenue engine; the South and Midwest have stronger margins at lower volume.
  • Online is the clearest growth lever. In-store still dominates, but online grew fastest after COVID.
  • Retailers are concentrated. West Gear and Foot Locker lead; Amazon and Walmart are under-used.
  • Profit leaders differ by measure. Men's Street Footwear leads total profit; apparel leads profit per unit.
  • Timing is predictable. Sales recovered strongly in 2021, and Q3 remained the peak season even through COVID.

My contribution · Test before scaling

An A/B testing plan

Historical data shows correlation, not cause. So instead of recommending a full budget shift, I designed four experiments to validate each strategy first:

TestA vs BSuccess metrics
Online growthFree shipping vs 10% offConversion, order value, units
Regional growthNational campaign vs a South-specific oneSales uplift, operating margin
Product positioningDiscount-led vs premium lifestyle messaging for apparelProfit per unit, order value
Retailer activationStandard listing vs sponsored placement + bundleRetailer sales growth, profit

The point. Move Adidas from describing what happened to deciding with evidence: test small, then scale what works.
