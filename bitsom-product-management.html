What I took away

Six lessons I now use

1 · Start from the job, not the feature
People "hire" a product to make progress. In the capstone, users didn't want a diagnosis; they wanted a clear next step in their own language.
2 · An MVP is what you leave out
Personas and MoSCoW/RICE made the cuts obvious: no diagnosis, no video consultations, no medical records. Just guidance and escalation, done well.
3 · Agentic AI is orchestration, not a chatbot
The value is in the steps around the model: detect intent, assess risk, retrieve only from verified sources (RAG), and hand over to a human. Responsible-AI boundaries are product requirements, not legal footnotes, and no-code tools let a PM prototype the flow in days.
4 · Lead without authority
Scrum ceremonies, a phased plan and a RACI kept a four-person team moving without anyone being "the boss".
5 · Distribution beats features
The best product fails if people never open it. That's why I proposed building on WhatsApp: in India it's already on almost every smartphone, so there's nothing new to download or learn.
6 · Pick metrics that fit the product
A health tool is used when needed, not daily. I measured trust and safe outcomes (correct escalation, following the advice) instead of vanity engagement.
